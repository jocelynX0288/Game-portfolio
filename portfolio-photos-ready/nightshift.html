Selected Work / Game Design

NightShift.

An asymmetric two-player horror game where one player monitors invading termites while the other assembles furniture and uses physical switches to defend the house.

Co-op HorrorAsymmetric GameplayPhysical Interaction

Project Details

Role
Game Designer & Artist
Team
5 people
Engine
Unity 6
Genre
Cooperative Horror / Puzzle
Duration
2 weeks

Gameplay Mechanics

01

Asymmetric Cooperation

Two players take on different roles and must communicate to coordinate their actions.

02

Spatial Puzzle

Players must strategically rearrange furniture to reach the correct door switch.

03

Escalating Threats

Each wave introduces more termites attacking from different directions.

My contribution

  • Contributed to asymmetric cooperative gameplay mechanics and physical-digital interactions.
  • Created termite character assets and animations using Maya.
  • Designed gameplay interfaces, visual feedback, and player guidance.
  • Developed a step-by-step tutorial to help players learn the mechanics and physical controls.
