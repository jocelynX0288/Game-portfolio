Selected Work / Game Design

Lightbound.

A VR puzzle adventure where players help a little fairy rescue her trapped parents from a mysterious cave by solving candle-lighting puzzles and fending off spiders.

VRPuzzleAdventure

Project Details

Role
Game Designer & Environment Artist
Platform
Meta Quest 3
Engine
Unity
Genre
VR Puzzle Adventure

Gameplay Mechanics

01

Light-Based Puzzles

Follow glowing clues on the cave walls to identify and light the correct candles.

02

Sequence Puzzles

Light candles in the correct order to free the fairy’s father.

03

Spider Defense

Use candlelight to repel attacking spiders while solving puzzles.

My contribution

  • Contributed to the design of candle-lighting puzzles and gameplay interactions.
  • Created the cave environment, including the cave structure, cages, and crystals.
  • Developed the cave’s visual atmosphere through lighting and HDRI environment design.
Torch Prop
The Quest controller fits inside a physical torch prop that players hold during the experience.
